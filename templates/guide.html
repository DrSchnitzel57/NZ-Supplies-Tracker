Quick-start guide (one page)

You need only a browser link and an account. No Docker, no terminal, no technical setup. All names and numbers below are invented examples.

1. Sign in

[ Sign in ] Username: [ staff-ava ] Password: [ •••••••• ] [ Sign in ] Your name appears at the top; choose “Sign out” when you leave a shared computer.

Ask an administrator for your account. If your sign-in is about to expire (30 minutes without clicking), a warning appears at the top — save your work and sign in again in a new tab.

2. Issue goods

[ Issue goods ] (the highlighted button at the top) Person: [ Synthetic Person A (EXT-0001) ▾ ] Item 1: [ Hygiene / Soap Bar — Available: 12 ▾ ] Qty [ 2 ] Item 2: [ — Choose — ▾ ] Qty [ ] [ Issue goods ]

Open Issue goods, choose the person and items, then choose “Issue goods”. Repeating the same item just adds the quantities. If stock is short, nothing is issued — you will see “You asked for / Available now / Revised quantity” and must confirm. Your draft stays on the page if anything fails.

3. Add stock

Inventory → “Hygiene / Soap Bar” → [ Add stock ] Quantity to add: [ 10 ] Reason: [ delivery from supplier ]

Open Inventory, open the item, fill in “Add stock” with a quantity and a reason, and choose “Add stock”.

4. Undo a mistake

History → Issue to Synthetic Person A → [ Undo this issue ] Reason: [ wrong person selected ] [ Undo this issue ]

Open History, open the issue, give a reason and choose “Undo this issue”. Everything in that issue goes back to Available. An issue can only be undone once; the original record is kept.

Tips

  • Available is what is on the shelf. “Given out” counts only current issues.
  • Numbers refresh every ~15 seconds without touching your form. If “Connection: offline” appears, keep your draft and retry — nothing is sent in the background.
  • Use a computer or phone browser: tables stack into readable cards on narrow screens, and every field works with keyboard (Tab / Enter).
