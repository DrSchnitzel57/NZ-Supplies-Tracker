Help & quick-start

The four everyday jobs. Pictures use made-up names and numbers.

{% include "components/icon.html" with name="plus" %}Issue supplies

Give supplies to a missionary

  1. Choose Issue supplies and type a name — “Sister Halvorsen”, a first name, or the missionary ID — then pick them from the list.
  2. Check the card: it shows their mission, when they leave, and what they’ve already received.
  3. Click a size (or Add) for each item. Use the category buttons or the search box to find things.
  4. Check the list on the right, change any quantities, then choose Issue supplies.

If something ran out while you were working, nothing is issued — you’ll see what’s short and can confirm the rest or go back.

Add stock or fix a count

  • A delivery arrived: in Inventory, choose Add stock on the item, enter how many and where they came from.
  • After a stocktake: choose Count and enter what is actually on the shelf. The difference is recorded with your reason.
  • New kind of item: Add an item (or Add several items for a delivery of many new lines).

Keep an eye on things

  • The Overview shows who is in the MTC, who leaves in the next two weeks (and whether they’ve received anything), what was given out this week, and what needs reordering.
  • Reorder list turns the low-stock list into an email you can copy or open in your email app.
  • Someone arrived? In Missionaries, set Arrived to Not yet arrived and choose Check in on their row (or on their page). It records today as their arrival date.
  • Numbers update by themselves every 15 seconds.

Undo a mistake, find anything

  • Gave something to the wrong person? Open it in History, give a reason and choose Undo this issue. Everything goes back on the shelf; the record stays, marked “Undone”.
  • Use the search box at the top of every page to jump to any missionary or item.
  • Reports download as a spreadsheet or an A4 PDF.

Good to know

  • Everyone shares the same live stock, so two people can issue at once without double-counting. Stock can never go below zero.
  • You’re signed out after 30 minutes without activity. If that happens mid-task, keep the tab open, sign in again in a new tab as the same person, then come back and try again — your unsaved list is still there.
  • Unsaved work lives only in the tab you’re using. Closing or reloading the tab discards it.
  • If a message says success is “not confirmed” (for example the network dropped), check History in another tab before trying again, so nothing is issued twice.
  • Forgotten your password, or need an account for someone new? Ask an administrator.
