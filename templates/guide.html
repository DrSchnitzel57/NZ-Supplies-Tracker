Quick-start guide

Four everyday tasks. All examples below are synthetic.

You need only the browser link and your staff account. Use Home to see low stock; use Menu on a narrow screen to reach all sections and help.

1. Sign in

Ask an administrator for an account. A temporary password must be changed on first sign-in. Check your username at the top and sign out when leaving a shared computer.

2. Issue goods

Open Issue goods. Search then explicitly select a missionary. Choose a catalogue item and quantity, then Add to issue. Repeat as needed (up to 50 distinct items). Review the named summary, then Issue goods.

If stock is short, nothing is issued until you confirm revised quantities. Back without issuing keeps your original draft. Changing the recipient clears items and the note only after confirmation.

3. Add stock or correct a count

In Inventory, open the item. Use Add stock for a delivery, with the quantity and reason. Use Correct stock count for the shelf count you actually counted, with a reason. These never invent issues.

A stale-count error keeps your input but does not refresh the version automatically. Review current stock in another tab, then reopen the item and count again.

4. Undo a mistake

Open Issue goods → History, filter or page to the issue, then open it. Give a reason and choose Undo this issue. All goods in that issue return to Available, once only. Original names, quantities and staff remain in history.

Reports shows one report at a time. Its CSV and A4 PDF include all matching records with the same filters. Current net excludes all undone issues; Available stock is stock now.

Keep an unfinished draft safe

  • Drafts last only in this open tab. Reloading or closing discards them. There is no autosave, personal browser storage or offline queue.
  • After idle expiry, keep this tab open and sign in in another tab as the same staff member. Retry here: the app checks your identity and refreshes its security token, never submits automatically.
  • Offline or no reply? Keep the original request. Success may be uncertain; retry deliberately with the same key, or check History in another tab. Do not start a duplicate issue.
  • Numbers refresh about every 15 seconds without changing your input or extending idle sign-in. Ask an administrator if you are unsure.
